05 — Open Questions & Roadmap

Co-Design Session Agenda and Phased Implementation Plan

Structured operational decisions to finalize between the Research Lead and Infrastructure Lead.

This document frames the concrete co-design agenda for our working session, structured across four policy groups, followed by a phased engineering roadmap.

Group A: Contribution Metrics & CRediT Tagging

Decision Item 1

Mechanisms for Scoring Effort: Raw event volume (Discord messages, GitHub commits) biases credit toward chattiness over architectural insight. We propose qualitative CRediT role tagging during milestone reviews, grounded by the event ledger. Does the Research Lead approve milestone role tagging as the primary attribution signal?

Group B: Privacy, Redaction, & Email Ingestion

Decision Item 2

Consent Posture for Email: Ingesting personal mailboxes creates privacy risks for untracked third parties. We propose Mode 1 (explicit CC to archive@project.org) with automated sender scrubbing. Is this posture sufficient for lab workflows?

Group C: Editorial Publication Gates

Decision Item 3

Authorization for Public Export: What quorum is required before an internal lab thread is bundled into an RO-Crate and deposited to Zenodo with a public DOI? (Recommended: Project lead or designated co-lead authorization).

Group D: Pilot Selection & Deployment Scope

Decision Item 4

Phase 1 Deployment Scope: Selecting active Stanford neuroscience benchmark workstreams (Sébastien Proulx) and one Open Neuromorphic community working group to run the pilot pipeline and validate empirical workflows.
